Projects

A showcase of my data analysis work

Olist E-Commerce Late Delivery Analysis & Prediction

Late deliveries (6.7% of orders) dropped review scores from about 4.2 to 2.3. Analyzed about 100,000 orders in a team of three, confirmed the effect with Welch's t-test (p < 0.001), and compared 12 leakage-free classification models. The selected model flagged 87% of truly late orders and, in simulation, lifted the estimated average review score from 3.01 to 3.98 (+32.26%). Deployed as a Streamlit app with a Tableau dashboard.

Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis & ML

Calling a bank's entire customer list wasted call-center hours. Led the modeling in a team of two, comparing 14 classification models and selecting Gradient Boosting (80% recall, 63% precision at a 0.351 cutoff). In a simulation with illustrative costs, the model-guided call list cut calls by 41% (about $9,555 saved) and converted 36% better than random dialing.

Unsynchronized Delivery to Installation Analysis

At Home & Living Company, 66.8% of furniture installations breached their 1-day SLA. In a team of two, cleaned and merged 300,000 order records and used ANOVA and Welch's t-tests to find a uniform 2.51-day scheduling gap from a systemic scheduling-engine issue, with an estimated Rp 54.88 billion exposure concentrated in Wardrobes. Proposed a central scheduling fix and targeted supplier audits.
